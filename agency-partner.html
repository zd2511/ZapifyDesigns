A straightforward process

Good collaboration starts with clarity.

Zapify is an independent business, so each partnership is assessed around availability, scope, fit and timelines. We'll discuss confidentiality, client communication, deliverables, payment terms and ownership before work starts.

  • Share your business, portfolio or profile
  • Explain the type of support you need or can offer
  • Discuss the working model and project scope
  • Agree on terms before committing

What to include in your enquiry

Help us understand whether the collaboration is a good fit by including:

  • Your name and business or freelance role
  • Website, portfolio or examples of work
  • Services you need or skills you offer
  • Project timeline and expected workload
  • Preferred collaboration model

Common questions

Before we team up.

Do you work with both agencies and freelancers?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome from agencies looking for project support and freelancers interested in collaboration.

Is white-label work available?

White-label or behind-the-scenes work can be discussed per project. Client ownership, confidentiality and communication expectations should be agreed in writing first.

Do you guarantee availability?

No. Each enquiry is assessed against the project scope, timeline and current capacity before any commitment is made.
