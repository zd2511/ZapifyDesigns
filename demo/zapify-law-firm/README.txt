Files:

- styles.css: responsive styling
- script.js: featured practice carousel, mobile navigation, contact form email handoff

HOW TO PREVIEW
Open index.html in a modern browser. Google Fonts and the photographic backgrounds are loaded from external URLs, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.

CONTACT FORM NOTE
The form validates required fields and opens the visitor's default email app with a prepared message addressed to hello@zapifylaw.co.za. It does not send or store submissions itself. Replace that placeholder address with the firm's real inbox before publishing.

CONTENT NOTE
Zapify Law Firm is a website concept. Replace contact details and confirm all practice areas, credentials, privacy language, and legal disclaimers with the firm's actual information before publishing. No legal qualifications or claims are implied.
