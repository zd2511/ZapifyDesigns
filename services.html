How quotes work

Every project is different. The starting price helps show that Zapify aims to be accessible, while the final quote reflects the real work involved.

Is every service R350?

No. R350 is a starting price for suitable entry-level work. Larger websites, e-commerce, custom applications and integrations are quoted according to scope.

Do you offer hosting?

An optional hosting service is available at R125 per month and includes website maintenance, subject to the applicable terms. Ask us to confirm suitability for your site.

Can you help with an existing website?

Yes. Share the website address and explain what you want to change or fix. We’ll review it and confirm what is feasible.
